Our Mission

Thread Together is a youth-led non-profit initiative dedicated to providing accessible, hands-on sewing and embroidery education. We foster creativity, sustainability, and community connections across the Greater Toronto Area.

About Us

Founded by passionate young creators, our goal is to pass down functional textile skills to the next generation while creating inclusive spaces for creative expression.
